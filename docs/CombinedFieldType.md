# CombinedFieldType

How a combined field joins its fields' values: with `combined_field_separator` between them (`separator`), or into `combined_field_format` (`format`).

## Enum

* `SEPARATOR` (value: `'separator'`)

* `FORMAT` (value: `'format'`)

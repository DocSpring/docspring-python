# ImageScaleType

How an image is resized to its field: scaled to fit inside it (`fit`), scaled to cover it and cropped (`fill`), or stretched to its shape (`stretch`).

## Enum

* `FIT` (value: `'fit'`)

* `FILL` (value: `'fill'`)

* `STRETCH` (value: `'stretch'`)

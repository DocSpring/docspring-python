# GenerateFilesFormat

A format DocSpring can add to a processed submission: `pdf`, `docx` or `odt` (a Word template's submission's PDF, Word document or OpenDocument file), or `png` (images of the PDF's pages).

## Enum

* `PDF` (value: `'pdf'`)

* `DOCX` (value: `'docx'`)

* `ODT` (value: `'odt'`)

* `PNG` (value: `'png'`)
